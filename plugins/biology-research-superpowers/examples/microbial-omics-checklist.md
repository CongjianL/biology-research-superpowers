# Example 3 — Microbial multi-omics study (illustrative)

- **Unit**: species, isolate, community sample or biological culture; list repeated measures/blocks.
- **Measurements**: counts, abundance, expression or protein intensities; document platform and detection limits.
- **QC**: blank/negative controls, contamination, batch/sample metadata joins, outliers (with predeclared rules).
- **Model**: sampling/covariates, normalization and compositionality, primary contrasts, multiple testing, effect sizes and uncertainty.
- **Validation**: replicate robustness, independent cohorts, negative controls and an orthogonal assay when feasible.
- **Conclusions**: association or differential abundance does not automatically prove mechanism or causality.

Suggested skills: `omics-statistics`, `sequence-data-curation`, `functional-assay-design`, `figure-evidence-audit`.
