# Example 1 — Microbial rhodopsin family evolution (illustrative, not findings)

**Question:** How did ion-transport functional diversity originate across a microbial rhodopsin family, and how could it relate to environmental selection?

## Distinct claims and acceptable evidence

| Claim | Candidate method | What remains unverified |
|---|---|---|
| Rhodopsin subfamilies have distinct histories | Homology-defined MSA, topology testing, reconciliation, sensitivity checks | Branch-specific origin and uncertainty |
| A residue is associated with an ion-transport type | Stratified association, ancestral reconstruction, structure map, comparison with experimental references | Whether the residue is causal |
| A protein pumps Na+, H+ or Cl- | Verified functional assays with expression and ion/light controls | Transport in tested constructs/conditions only |
| The rhodopsin improves stress survival | Controlled organism/cell phenotype under specified stress, fitness readout | Cannot infer from transport assay alone |
| A change evolved as an adaptation to desert conditions | Comparative ecological context, phylogenetic non-independence, alternatives, fitness evidence | Requires stronger evidence than transport alone |

## Suggested skills

`sequence-data-curation` → `phylogenetic-inference` + `protein-family-evolution` → `structure-function-analysis` → `functional-assay-design` → `figure-evidence-audit` → `requesting-peer-review`.

## Quality gates

Check strain/genome provenance, branch/topology sensitivity, accession/structure numbering, bio vs technical replicate counts and exact assay outcomes. This is a conceptual plan; no biological data were acquired or analyzed by this example.
