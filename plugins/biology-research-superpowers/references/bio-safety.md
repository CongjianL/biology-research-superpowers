# Scope, safety and ethics for biological research

- Follow institutional biosafety rules, training, material transfer rules, field sampling permits, biodiversity protection, ethics/IRB and human subject consent as applicable.
- Avoid executing, publishing or escalating risky operational protocols without appropriate permissions or safety reviews. This plugin is intended for ordinary reproducible research and reporting, not unsupervised biological intervention.
- Keep personally identifying clinical or genotype data in approved controlled storage; do not upload them to public repositories without rights, consent and de-identification assessment.
- Species/pathogen identity and procedures can change the safety review requirements; check local institutional guidance rather than guessing a biosafety level.
- A SOFT-GATE approval is a scientific workflow audit, NOT a substitute for regulatory or biosafety authorization.
