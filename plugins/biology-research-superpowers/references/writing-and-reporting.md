# Biological research writing and reporting

- Cite a primary result with actual DOI/PMID/accession and precise figure/table/page context; never invent references, numbers or quotes.
- Methods must provide sample selection, biological n, controls, exclusions, preprocessing, software/database versions, models, parameters, random seeds, and statistical tests.
- Results must report numerator/denominator, interval estimates, adjusted p where relevant, and complete figure/table provenance.
- Conclusions must match the demonstrated evidence type: sequence prediction vs model inference vs controlled assay vs phenotype/evolutionary adaptation.
- PRISMA is for systematic reviews; STROBE for observational epidemiology; ARRIVE for animal research; MIAME/MINSEQE/other domain reporting standards may apply according to current journal policy. Do not force irrelevant guidelines.
- Include data and code availability, ethics/biosafety/consent where applicable, limitations, conflicts and methods deviations.
- Preprints, peer-reviewed papers and data records must be labeled distinctly, including correction and retraction checks.
